    What "wealth" and these measures mean

    Wealth of an agent = its money + the goods it holds valued at today's prices + its asset shares valued at today's asset prices. Asset prices come from profit (output value minus wages, divided by the return), so assets that don't cover their wages are worth 0 even though they still produce. Wealth is a snapshot at current prices: it changes when prices move, not only when things change hands.

    Why inequality moves slowly: everyone starts with the same money, and money is most of wealth, so the wealth Gini starts near 0 and only drifts as trades, wages and asset sales move money around. A few hundred steps of small trades barely move it. Starting with less money (or uneven money), or less stock, makes differences show up faster.

    Gini: 0 = everyone holds the same, 1 = one agent holds everything. Income and consumption inequality use each agent's smoothed per-step income/consumption. Asset ownership uses shares owned. Each figure below shows its change since the start of the window.

    Employment rate = agents who worked this step (on their own assets or hired). Participation = agents offering labour. Unemployment = labour offered that nobody hired. Unfilled jobs = jobs on assets employers wanted but could not fill. Labour share of output = wages paid ÷ value of goods produced (owners working their own assets aren't paid a wage).
